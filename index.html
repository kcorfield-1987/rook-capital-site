About

Long-term capital. Deliberate moves.

ROOK Capital is a private investment company and family office that invests in operating businesses and commercial real estate, with a focus on multifamily and industrial.

Our leadership brings more than 30 years of combined experience both developing real estate and building, running, and selling operating companies.

Having sat on both sides of the table, we bring that perspective to every investment, as patient capital and a hands-on partner.

What We Do

01

Real Estate Investment & Development

Ground-up development, value-add acquisitions, and long-term ownership of multifamily and industrial real estate.

02

Operating Businesses

Majority and minority investments in established, mid-market private companies, with patient capital for owners planning growth or succession.

03

Co-Investment & Partnerships

Co-investments and partnerships with sponsors, operators, and capital firms, participating as limited partner or co-general partner.

04

Advisory & Asset Management

Real estate investment consultancy and property management for our partners, co-investors, and the assets we own.
